Four lenses. Every build is assessed against all four — but the theme is the headline and everything else is in service of it.
LENS A — THEME: ACCESS TO JUSTICE
How much could this genuinely unlock access to justice in the UK? This is the headline. Everything else is in service of it.
LENS B — AGENTIC FIRST
Something beyond a chatbot in a UI — your solution does things in the real world. Drafting letters is fine; sending them is better.
LENS C — USER EXPERIENCE & PRODUCT
Not a terminal-demo competition. We want a full, breathing PoC — something a real consumer could plausibly use. Bonus marks for multi-modal interaction or meeting people where they already are (WhatsApp, voice, email).
LENS D — LEGALLY ACCURATE
The output has to feel like genuine legal advice — not generic AI waffle dressed up in legal language.
